I’m currently studying Information Systems and Management at the University of Auckland. After graduating, I plan to transition into cybersecurity at AUT. I enjoy problem solving, learning new technologies, and challenging myself through practical projects.

<h2>Projects 💻</h2>

[AuthScope](https://github.com/bkhammy/authscope-lab)

A home lab I built to better understand Windows security from both the attacker and defender perspectives. It uses Active Directory and Splunk to collect authentication logs, simulate a password spray attack, and detect suspicious activity through custom searches, alerts, and dashboards.

[HookTrace](https://github.com/bkhammy/hook-trace)

A phishing email analysis tool I built to inspect exported email files safely. It extracts email headers and suspicious indicators, explains why a message looks legitimate or malicious, and generates easy to read investigation reports without opening links or executing attachments.

<h2>Fun Projects 😺</h2>

[ClutchCircuit](https://github.com/bkhammy/clutch-circuit)

A fun project I built to simulate NBA games using live team and roster data. It predicts matchups with Monte Carlo simulations and generates a full play by play from the opening tip to the final buzzer.
